About Arvex Apparels

A manufacturing floor built around consistency, not just capacity.

Arvex Apparels was set up to solve a specific problem: brands finding a manufacturer who can hold quality steady from a 100-unit sample run to a 10,000-unit reorder. We work exclusively B2B, across two divisions — sportswear and streetwear — so every line on our floor is set up for the fabrics and finishing each category actually needs.

From pattern to packed carton, our team manages cutting, sewing, finishing, quality control and export documentation in-house, which means fewer handoffs and fewer places for a spec to drift.

How we hold quality steady.

Three checkpoints, on every order, regardless of size.

Checkpoint 01

Pre-production sample

Physical sample approved against spec before bulk cutting begins.

Checkpoint 02

In-line inspection

Random checks through cutting, sewing and finishing stages.

Checkpoint 03

Final audit before packing

Full AQL-based inspection before cartons are sealed for export.
